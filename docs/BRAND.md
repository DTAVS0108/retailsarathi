# RetailSarathi — Brand Decision

## Chosen Name: **RetailSarathi**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Product name** | RetailSarathi |
| **Tagline** | Your shop's sarathi — from billing to GST to repairs |
| **Domain** | **retailsarathi.in** (registered) |
| **API** | `https://api.retailsarathi.in` |
| **Package namespace** | `@retail-sarathi/*` |

## Rationale

- **Sarathi** (सारथी) means guide/companion — positions the app as a trusted partner for Indian retailers.
- Broader appeal than repair-only names; supports retail, wholesale, repair, and compliance modules.
- Works for single-store desktop users and future multi-branch SaaS.
